Learning through interaction

Building agents that learn from experience.

I am a master's student in Computer Science and Technology at Northeastern University. My research focuses on GUI agents, multimodal learning, and large language model post-training.

I am particularly interested in how agents explore real environments, turn interactions into useful training data, and reuse experience across tasks. My work connects data construction, model adaptation, and evaluation.
