About Me

I am a master's student in Computer Science and Technology at Northeastern University, China. I received my bachelor's degree in Artificial Intelligence from Wuhan Textile University in 2026. My research interests include GUI agents, multimodal learning, and large language model post-training.

I am currently an intern at Microsoft's Sico Lab. I work on multi-agent execution, experience reuse, and GUI agent post-training. I am particularly interested in how agents explore real environments, turn interactions into training data, and learn from experience across tasks.
